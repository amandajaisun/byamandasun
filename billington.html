Overview

Windowless rooms are endemic in our buildings and have real, negative impacts on their inhabitants.

Under Basma Altaif, Sarah Billington, and Elizabeth Murnane, I translated a biophilic research paper’s discoveries into Processing (opens in a new tab) code.

Inspiration

“Can natural cues in windowless rooms help humans tell time?”

Our goal was to measure this through a projection of a tree whose branches moved with the same wind speed that was recorded outside. Thus, we had to create an auto-generative tree that could change, have a dynamic wind speed, and have multiple iterations.

  1. Outside

    The ESP32 detects windspeed changes outside, sending information via wifi.

  2. Tree

    Processing picks up the information via wifi, and changes the animation projected indoors to match the changing conditions outside.
